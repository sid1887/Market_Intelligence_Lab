## 4. Quantitative Methodology & Mathematical Formulations

### 1. Volume Ratio vs. Volume Z-Score
$$\text{VR}_{t, 20} = \frac{V_t}{\frac{1}{20}\sum_{i=1}^{20} V_{t-i}}, \qquad Z_{V, t, 50} = \frac{V_t - \mu_{V, 50}}{\sigma_{V, 50}}, \qquad Z_{\ln V, t, 50} = \frac{\ln(V_t) - \mu_{\ln V}}{\sigma_{\ln V}}$$
Where historical volume series are scaled by corporate action multipliers: $V_{\text{adj}, t} = V_t / \prod_{\tau > t} k_\tau$.

### 2. Rolling Beta Formulation
$$\beta_{60d} = \frac{\text{Cov}(R_{\text{stock}}, R_{\text{benchmark}})}{\text{Var}(R_{\text{benchmark}})} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{60} (R_{s, i} - \bar{R}_s)(R_{b, i} - \bar{R}_b)}{\sum_{i=1}^{60} (R_{b, i} - \bar{R}_b)^2}$$
Evaluated strictly on aligned trading session pairs with identical economic return modes.

### 3. Advance / Decline Volume Breadth
$$\text{Volume Breadth Ratio} = \frac{\sum_{i \in \text{Advancing}} V_{i, t}}{\sum_{j \in \text{Declining}} V_{j, t}}$$
